These terms apply when you request a translation from Attesta Translation ("Attesta", "we", "us") through attestatranslation.com, by email or by WhatsApp. By sending us documents or paying for an order, you agree to them.

What we do

We translate documents from Portuguese, Spanish and French into English, and Italian into English when we confirm it before you pay. Each translation comes with a signed Certificate of Translation Accuracy stating that the translator is competent in both languages and that the translation is complete and accurate. This is the kind of certification described in 8 CFR 103.2(b)(3) for documents filed with USCIS.

What we do not do

  • We are not a law firm. We do not give legal or immigration advice and cannot tell you which documents your case needs. Ask your attorney or accredited representative.
  • We are not approved by USCIS. USCIS does not approve or certify translators or translation companies. We do not claim otherwise.
  • We do not decide outcomes. We cannot promise that any agency, court, school or employer will accept a translation or how your case will turn out.
  • Our translations are certified, not sworn. A certified translation for a U.S. filing is different from a sworn translation (tradução juramentada, traduction assermentée, traducción jurada) made by a translator appointed in another country. Tell us before you order if you need a sworn translation. We will say whether we can help.

Your responsibilities

  • Send complete, readable copies of every page, including the back of any page that has writing, stamps or notes. Tell us if something is unclear in the original.
  • Send only documents you have the right to send. Do not send forged or altered documents. We may refuse or stop any order that appears to involve one.
  • Review the finished translation, especially names, dates, numbers and places, before you file it. Tell us right away about anything that looks wrong.
  • If a name appears in the original in a different spelling than in your other records (for example on a passport), tell us. We translate what the document says and keep names as written.

How the translation is prepared

We translate everything on the page, including stamps and notes in the margins. Names, brand names and codes stay as they appear in the original. Signatures, stamps, seals, logos and QR codes that cannot be translated are described in brackets, for example [Signature] or [Stamp: Name / Title]. Where a document contains Brazilian Reais (BRL), we show an approximate U.S. Dollar figure next to each amount. The BRL figure stays primary, and the USD figure is for convenience only. We aim for the translated pages to look like the original: same layout, same order of information. Some fonts or decorative elements may differ.

Prices, pages and payment

The price is shown on our site before you order: $15.00 per page, and $12.50 per page for orders of more than 10 pages. A page is up to 250 words. We count the pages and confirm the total with you before you pay. Rush service, notarization and a mailed printed copy are available on request at the price we quote you. We start work when payment is received. We accept cards, through a secure payment link we send you, and Zelle. Zelle payments cannot be reversed by us or by your bank, so check that the recipient name matches the one in our payment instructions before you send. Prices are in U.S. Dollars.

Turnaround

Standard orders are delivered in about 2 business days after payment, and rush orders in about 24 hours, when your documents are readable and complete. These are estimates, not guarantees. Large orders, poor-quality scans and unclear handwriting can take longer, and we will tell you if so.

Delivery and signature

We deliver a PDF containing the translation, the Certificate of Translation Accuracy and a copy of your original, by email unless we agree otherwise.
